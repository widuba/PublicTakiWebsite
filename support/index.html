Frequently asked questions

How do I use Taki?

Just say or type what you want in plain language — “remind me to call Mom at 6,” “what's the weather this weekend,” or “track my flight UA123.” Tap the microphone for hands-free voice mode.

What are credits, and what do subscriptions include?

AI Credits pay the existing variable cost of each request. A voice request uses those normal AI Credits plus one Voice Credit. If no Voice Credits remain, voice stays available for the normal AI cost plus 40 AI Credits. Plus includes 4,000 AI Credits and 50 Voice Credits monthly. Premium includes 6,000 AI Credits and 300 Voice Credits monthly. Pro includes 12,000 AI Credits and 600 Voice Credits monthly. Taki checks the entire charge first, so a request never partially charges either balance.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

For an App Store subscription, open iOS Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → Taki AI Assistant. For a plan purchased on takiai.app through Stripe, email Taki AI Support from the purchase email and include your Account ID. Cancelling stops future renewals; access continues through the paid period.

How do I change the assistant's voice?

Start voice mode, tap Change Voice at the top, and swipe through the voices — each one previews aloud. Tap Select to keep one. You can also change it in Settings.

Is my data private?

Only information needed for a feature you request is processed. Taki does not upload your device libraries wholesale, use your data for advertising, or sell it. Specific content needed to answer a request may be sent securely to Taki's service providers. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Voice mode isn't hearing me correctly.

Make sure Taki has microphone permission (iOS Settings → Taki AI Assistant → Microphone). In noisy places, pausing clearly when you finish speaking helps it know you're done.

How do I delete my data?

In Taki AI, open Settings, choose Data and privacy, then Delete account. Taki will ask for multiple confirmations and fresh Apple authentication before permanently deleting the account. You can also contact Taki AI Support or revoke device permissions anytime in iOS Settings.
